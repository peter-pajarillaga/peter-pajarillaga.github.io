Before we begin

Your questions,
answered.

When and where is RISE Maui 2027?

January 19–23, 2027, at Lumeria Maui Wellness Center in Maui, Hawaii.

Who is this retreat for?

RISE is designed for purpose-driven health practitioners and leaders looking for restoration, connection and sustainable personal and professional growth.

What will we explore?

The four RISE pillars: Restore, Integrate, Strategize and Empower, through practices, discussions and workshops described above.

Is there a shared-room option?

The updated flyer lists a shared-room rate of $2,547. Confirm room availability and arrangements with the registration provider.

How do I use the Early Bird coupon?

The retreat materials list the code MauiEarlyBird and an extended October 31, 2026 deadline. Coupon entry and eligibility must be confirmed at checkout.

Are travel, accommodation and meals included?

These inclusions are not fully specified in the supplied materials. Please confirm what your chosen registration includes before booking.

What is the cancellation or refund policy?

Please review the terms on the registration page or contact the retreat organizers before purchasing.
